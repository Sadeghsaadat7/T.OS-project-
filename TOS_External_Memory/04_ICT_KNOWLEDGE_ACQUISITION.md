# 04 — ICT Knowledge Acquisition System

## Mission
Reconstruct and document the trading knowledge taught by Michael Huddleston (ICT) as accurately as possible, using primary sources whenever possible.

**Objective:** reconstruct what ICT actually taught — not create an improved external interpretation.

## Primary source
Official ICT YouTube channel:
`https://www.youtube.com/@InnerCircleTrader`

## Research team

### 1. ICT Source Scout
Discover/inventory primary playlists and videos, chronology, duplicates/reuploads/archives, URLs, accessibility and missing sources.

### 2. ICT Curriculum Researcher
Study teaching systematically, preserve chronology and curriculum context, record concept introduction and evolution over time.

### 3. ICT Concept Researcher
Extract concepts, definitions, terminology, relationships, conditions, distinctions, examples, exceptions and explicitly stated rules. Never convert an example into a universal rule without evidence.

### 4. ICT Time & Price Researcher
Independently map time-related concepts, price-related concepts, timeframe relationships, temporal conditions, price conditions and explicit interactions between time and price.

### 5. ICT Model & Strategy Researcher
Map concept vs framework vs model vs setup vs entry condition vs confirmation vs invalidation vs example/application.

### 6. ICT Auditor / Truth Controller
Audit claims against primary sources; detect unsupported claims, terminology drift, contradictions and external contamination.

### 7. ICT Knowledge Architect
Integrate validated research into the Master ICT Knowledge Base, terminology dictionary, concept inventory, time framework, price framework, model/setup library, evolution map, evidence database and knowledge graph. It must not invent missing knowledge.

## Deliverables
1. Complete ICT Source Map
2. Complete ICT Concept Inventory
3. ICT Terminology Dictionary
4. ICT Time Framework
5. ICT Price Framework
6. ICT Models & Setups
7. ICT Evolution Map
8. ICT Evidence Database
9. ICT Knowledge Graph
10. Candidate / Final TOS Agent Inventory

## Operational definition of completeness
Do not claim absolute completeness. Treat research as sufficiently complete only when discovered relevant primary playlists/videos are inventoried, knowledge domains have coverage matrices, material claims have evidence status, inaccessible material and gaps are listed, contradictions are preserved, and the eventual agent inventory is traceable to knowledge domains.

## Source hierarchy
1. Direct original ICT teaching
2. Official ICT material
3. Archived original teaching
4. Third-party archives/transcripts for discovery or verification
5. Third-party explanations only as secondary references

## Prohibited
No personal trading theory; no “improvement” of ICT; no silent merging with SMC/Wyckoff/Elliott Wave/etc.; no community consensus presented as doctrine; no invented rules, sources, timestamps or quotations; no pretending to have accessed unavailable material.
