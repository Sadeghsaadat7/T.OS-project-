# 03 — Analysis Layer

## Objective
Transform raw market information into a structured analytical state while preserving the distinctions required by the validated ICT knowledge base.

## A. Context / Content
Investigate higher-timeframe context, market condition, relevant reference points, relationships and model context. Exact terminology must come from ICT primary research.

## B. Time Alignment
Dedicated responsibility for temporal structure: relevant temporal framework, higher-timeframe schedule/context, sessions/time windows, timeframe relationships and temporal conditions required by a model.

Do not reduce Time Alignment to a generic “best time to trade” concept.

## C. Price Alignment
Dedicated responsibility for price: price structure, reference levels/zones, relation to context/model, confirmation/invalidation/refinement conditions.

## D. Multi-timeframe / Macro Review
Investigate macro, intermediate and lower timeframes; transitions and relationships between scales; time-price relationships across scales.

## Candidate research-derived categories
1. ICT Knowledge / Concept Retrieval
2. Macro Context
3. Time Alignment
4. Price Alignment
5. Multi-Timeframe Relationship
6. Model / Setup Recognition
7. Evidence / Audit

These are hypotheses, not the final architecture. The final list must be derived after ICT research.

## Required analytical distinctions
Every important result should distinguish observed fact, ICT-supported interpretation, assumption, uncertainty, evidence, conclusion, validation state and handoff.
