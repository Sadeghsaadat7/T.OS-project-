# 10 — TOS Rules and Decisions

1. **Human engineer remains in control.** The user is owner, engineer and final monitor.
2. **Build layer by layer.** Do not prematurely mix analysis, risk, timing and monitoring.
3. **Analysis comes first.** It is the first major operational layer.
4. **Time and Price remain distinct.** Time Alignment and Price Alignment are separate analytical responsibilities unless validated research establishes a better architecture.
5. **Do not finalize the agent inventory prematurely.** Derive it from the completed ICT knowledge structure.
6. **Primary-source ICT fidelity.** Learn primarily from Michael Huddleston's original teaching.
7. **No external contamination.** Do not merge outside frameworks unless primary ICT evidence explicitly establishes the relationship.
8. **No hallucinated evidence.** Never invent sources, URLs, timestamps, quotations, definitions or rules.
9. **“I don't know” is valid.** Uncertainty is preferable to fabricated certainty.
10. **Evidence before interpretation.** Material conclusions must be traceable.
11. **Preserve conflicts.** If sources differ, preserve the difference and chronology rather than silently selecting one.
12. **Scope is contractual.** Agents must not perform other agents' responsibilities without authorization.
13. **Structured handoffs.** Downstream agents must receive predictable, auditable outputs.
14. **Version important artifacts.** Prompts, schemas, specifications and architecture decisions need versions.
15. **Iterate from failures.** Capture failure, classify it, fix the appropriate layer, retest and version.
