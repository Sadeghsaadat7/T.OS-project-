## 1. Purpose and Scope

This report establishes an evidence-based baseline of prior TOS
educational work before any new educational research or subsequent
design stage begins.

The review distinguishes between:

-   An artifact existing and the work it is intended to support being
    completed.
-   Source registration and source authenticity or accessibility.
-   Preliminary research and complete educational review.
-   Teaching extraction and independently validated educational
    knowledge.
-   A documented requirement and evidence that the requirement has been
    implemented.
-   A completed audit and a completed educational workstream.

The review is limited to accessible Library artifacts, available project
records, and the specifically inspected repository ZIP snapshot. It does
not claim to reconstruct every prior conversation, every historical
artifact, or the current complete state of GitHub.

**Overall determination: EDUCATIONAL WORKSTREAM INCOMPLETE / COVERAGE
NOT ESTABLISHED.**

**Disposition: PARTIAL --- REGISTRY COMPLETE; EDUCATIONAL COVERAGE NOT
ESTABLISHED.**

historical work. They should not be repeated blindly. Their actual
outputs, evidence, and acceptance status must be reconciled before
deciding what work needs to be performed again.

-   Which historical teaching-extraction artifacts actually exist?

-   Were explicit teachings distinguished from interpretations,
    inferences, and hypotheses?
-   Was independent educational QC performed?
-   Was coverage assessed against the entire defined Core Content scope?
-   Which registry limitations remain unresolved?
