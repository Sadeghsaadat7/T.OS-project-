This document records the result of a bounded follow-up review of prior
educational work artifacts. It builds on the user-confirmed baseline:

`TOS_Educational_Workstream_Baseline_Evidence_Reconciliation_v1.0.md`

## 5. Classification Rules

-   **CONFIRMED / COMPLETE WITHIN SCOPE:** supported by a specific
    reviewed artifact, within its documented limits.
-   **PARTIAL:** some activity or deliverable is evidenced, but
    completion criteria are not established.
-   **UNVERIFIED:** the evidence reviewed is insufficient to determine
    the status; this does not mean the work definitely never occurred.
-   **NOT ESTABLISHED:** the reviewed evidence does not support a
    completion claim.
-   **PRESERVE:** retain an existing artifact or historical work; do not
    recreate it without an evidence-based reason.
