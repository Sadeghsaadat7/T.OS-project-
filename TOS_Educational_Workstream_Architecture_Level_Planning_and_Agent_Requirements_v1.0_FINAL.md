## 5. Architecture-Level Coverage Model

Future tracking must keep these dimensions distinct for each source group and, where applicable, each source record:

1. **Inventory:** the source is represented in an identifiable list.
2. **Provenance and identity:** evidence supports that the item is the intended official or otherwise approved source.
3. **Accessibility and integrity:** the item can be accessed and its relevant file/version identity is sufficiently clear.
4. **Review status:** the source has or has not been reviewed by an authorized educational Agent.
5. **Extraction status:** the required educational output has or has not been produced.
6. **Evidence traceability:** each attributed teaching or claim can be traced to the relevant source location.
7. **Epistemic classification:** source teaching is kept distinct from interpretation, inference, hypothesis, and unverified material.
8. **Ambiguity and contradiction handling:** uncertainties, conflicting evidence, limitations, and unresolved questions remain visible.
9. **Independent quality control:** output has or has not passed the defined QC and coverage checks.
10. **Acceptance and integration:** only appropriately validated material is accepted and integrated through existing TOS controls.

A source being listed does not prove it was reviewed. A source being reviewed does not prove extraction is complete. An extracted item does not prove it passed QC. A QC result does not by itself prove corpus-wide coverage or controlled integration.

## 6. Future Agent Responsibilities — Requirements Only

These are functional responsibility areas for later Agent design, not final Agent definitions or prompts. Whether they become separate Agents or grouped responsibilities is a later design decision.

### A. Source Inventory and Reconciliation responsibility
- Maintain a traceable inventory by source group.
- Compare new evidence with existing registries and historical records.
- Flag missing identifiers, provenance issues, access problems, possible duplicates, and metadata discrepancies.
- Preserve unresolved items without guessing or silently overwriting prior records.

### B. Educational Review and Extraction responsibility
- Review educational sources only when authorized and in the approved sequence.
- Produce structured outputs traceable to the exact source.
- Distinguish explicit source teaching from interpretation, inference, hypothesis, and uncertainty.
- Record ambiguity, contradictions, limitations, and unanswered questions.
- Avoid treating a summary or partial review as complete corpus coverage.

### C. Evidence and Knowledge Validation responsibility
- Check whether claims attributed to a source are supported by traceable evidence.
- Keep source evidence separate from conclusions and assumptions.
- Identify unsupported attribution, conflicts, and gaps requiring further evidence.
- Apply the existing TOS validation and controlled-change boundaries; do not autonomously change doctrine or architecture.

### D. Independent QC and Coverage responsibility
- Independently assess output quality, traceability, classification, contradiction handling, and required coverage.
- Compare expected scope with completed and accepted scope.
- Report pass/fail/partial/unverified outcomes with reasons and evidence.
- Avoid approving its own work where independent control is required.

### E. Knowledge Integration and Learning-Record responsibility
- Prepare only QC-accepted material for controlled integration.
- Preserve source links, evidence status, limitations, and change history.
- Keep learning records separate from automatic doctrine changes.
- Route unresolved decisions to the authorized human/system controls.

These responsibility areas are not an authorization to design or instantiate Agents now.

## 7. Requirements to Prepare Before Future Master Prompt and Specialized Instructions

The following requirements must be made explicit before prompt design begins. This section is a readiness checklist, not prompt engineering.

- **Authority:** Human Authority / System Owner retains final approval and change authority.
- **Scope:** each future task must identify source group, bounded corpus, objective, and exclusions.
- **Inputs:** accepted source registry, source files/URLs, prior outputs, and relevant TOS requirements must be identifiable.
- **Source hierarchy:** the official Michael Huddleston YouTube channel is the primary reference for his video teachings; secondary indexes may support discovery but do not independently establish authenticity or teaching attribution.
- **Output contract:** expected record structure, source locator, status fields, uncertainty log, and completion report must be specified.
- **Evidence standard:** claims must be traceable; missing evidence must remain missing rather than being inferred.
- **Classification:** source teaching, interpretation, inference, hypothesis, and unverified material must be distinct.
- **Conflict handling:** contradictions and source/version discrepancies must be recorded and escalated according to existing TOS controls.
- **QC and acceptance:** acceptance criteria, independent-review expectations, coverage accounting, and rejection/rework outcomes must be observable.
- **Change control:** no silent modification of approved knowledge, frozen architecture, or prior records; changes require traceable justification and appropriate authorization.
- **Boundaries:** no automatic promotion of extracted material to validated doctrine; no unauthorized research cycle or operational action.
- **Completion reporting:** distinguish complete, partial, unverified, not started, and blocked only when evidence supports the classification.
- **Resource constraints:** work should be bounded and targeted; stop once the required evidence is sufficient, and avoid duplicate broad searches.
- **Human escalation:** specify which unresolved source, evidence, scope, or acceptance decisions require Human Authority input.
