## 1. Purpose

Determine whether the current educational source inventory, evidence
baseline, architecture-level planning and documented limitations are
sufficient to proceed to bounded Educational Agent Design.

This assessment does not perform detailed Agent design, ICT research,
prompt engineering, coding or implementation.

## 2. Governing Baseline

The assessment reuses:

-   Educational Workstream Architecture-Level Planning and Agent
    Requirements v1.0.
-   Historical Educational Work Artifact Reconciliation v1.0.
-   Educational Workstream Completion Audit v1.0.
-   Educational Source Inventory and Evidence Readiness v1.0.
-   Existing approved TOS architecture and knowledge-validation
    controls.

Completed work must be preserved. No previous artifact or registry may
be unnecessarily recreated.

## 3. Current Evidence Position

### 3.1 Established

-   Architecture-level educational planning is completed.
-   High-level educational coverage dimensions are defined.
-   Functional responsibility areas for future Agents are documented.
-   The 115-record Core Content registry exists and is reusable.
-   Existing TOS source-traceability, validation, uncertainty and
    controlled-integration requirements are available.
-   Historical educational work and its limitations are documented.

### 3.2 Partial or Unverified

-   Complete Core Content provenance and educational coverage.
-   Mentorships collection completeness and file equivalence.
-   Latest Videos publication cutoff and complete inventory.
-   Complete historical educational output inventory and acceptance.
-   Corpus-wide educational QC.
-   Controlled knowledge-integration evidence.

### 3.3 Not Established

Complete educational source coverage, validated educational corpus, and
completed knowledge integration.

## 4. Readiness Assessment

The outstanding limitations primarily concern evidence, source coverage
and educational execution.

They do not demonstrate an architectural deficiency or establish that
detailed Agent design is impossible.

The existing high-level coverage model and future Agent responsibility
requirements provide a sufficient foundation for bounded design.

However, Agent design must explicitly accommodate incomplete source
inventories, uncertain provenance, missing historical outputs,
unresolved contradictions and unverified educational coverage.

## 5. Mandatory Design Constraints

Future Educational Agents must be designed to:

1.  Preserve the distinction between source teaching, interpretation,
    inference and hypothesis.
2.  Maintain traceability to identifiable source material.
3.  Identify incomplete, inaccessible or uncertain inputs.
4.  Preserve unresolved discrepancies without unsupported assumptions.
5.  Distinguish inventory, review, extraction, QC, acceptance and
    integration status.
6.  Prevent partial work from being reported as complete coverage.
7.  Maintain independent quality-control responsibilities.
8.  Respect existing TOS validation and controlled-change requirements.
9.  Preserve prior approved records and educational artifacts.
10. Escalate consequential unresolved decisions to Human Authority.

These are mandatory design constraints, not final Agent definitions or
prompts.

## 6. Preliminary Action Assessment

No additional broad source-reconciliation exercise is required as a
mandatory prerequisite to bounded Agent design.

The existing limitations are sufficiently documented to be managed as
explicit design inputs and future Agent responsibilities.

This decision does not eliminate the requirement to resolve those
limitations during the subsequent educational execution stages.

Any additional targeted investigation must have a specific evidence
objective and documented justification.

## 7. Final Readiness Determination

**RESULT: CONDITIONALLY READY FOR BOUNDED EDUCATIONAL AGENT DESIGN.**

The current evidence and planning baseline is sufficient to begin the
next distinct design stage, subject to:

-   Explicit management of incomplete and unverified inputs.
-   Preservation of existing work.
-   Clear separation of Agent responsibilities and quality controls.
-   Retention of Human Authority decision and acceptance rights.
-   No unsupported claims of educational completeness.

This determination does not establish educational execution readiness,
complete source coverage, or completed knowledge integration.
