# TOS Educational Workstream Completion Audit v1.0

**Status:** FINAL — AWAITING GITHUB UPLOAD  
**Audit date:** 2026-10-25  
**Workstream:** Educational Workstream Completion Audit  
**Authority:** Human Authority / System Owner

## 1. Executive Summary

This audit determines what the available evidence establishes about TOS educational work before any subsequent design stage.

The Core Content source registry is a completed, user-confirmed deliverable within its documented structural scope. It contains 115 records. Its completion does **not** establish that all sources are authentic and accessible, that their content has been reviewed, or that their teachings have been extracted, validated, quality-controlled, and integrated into TOS.

Earlier project records refer to exploratory research on a limited set of ICT videos and a limited pilot involving Core Content video 01. The available evidence does not demonstrate completion of the full educational workflow or comprehensive coverage of any of the three required source groups.

**Overall determination: EDUCATIONAL WORKSTREAM COMPLETION NOT ESTABLISHED.**

- Core Content source registry and structural checks: **COMPLETE within documented scope**.
- Core Content full-corpus provenance, availability, and content review: **PARTIAL / UNVERIFIED**.
- Core Content teaching extraction and validation across all 115 records: **NOT ESTABLISHED**.
- Mentorships inventory and educational coverage: **UNVERIFIED**.
- Latest official-channel video inventory and educational coverage: **UNVERIFIED**.
- Full-corpus QC, coverage assessment, and controlled knowledge integration: **NOT ESTABLISHED**.

This is a status audit, not authorization to start a new research cycle. It does not select or design an Agent and does not reopen frozen architecture.

These are historical findings. Their present resolution status remains **UNVERIFIED** unless new, reviewable evidence establishes closure.

### E-03 — Earlier ICT Research Records

Earlier project records refer to exploratory work on a limited set of 2022 Introduction / Episodes 2–5 videos. The available record indicates that some titles and identities were checked, while substantive claim extraction and primary-source confirmation remained incomplete or pending.

A later handoff also refers to a limited pilot involving Core Content video 01. The available evidence does not establish that this pilot produced a complete, independently reviewed teaching reconstruction, passed full educational QC, or demonstrated corpus-wide coverage.

The full set and current status of all historical research artifacts have not been reconstructed. Therefore, undocumented work is not presumed absent, but it cannot be marked complete without evidence.

### E-04 — Core Control Prompt Validation

Artifact: `TOS_Limited_Core_Prompt_Validation_Report_v1.0.md`

The report records seven simulated behavioral tests passing and AC-01–AC-11 as PASS, with a note on AC-06. This supports only the documented limited prompt-behavior evaluation. It is not evidence of ICT source review or educational knowledge validation.

### E-05 — Existing TOS Requirements and Architecture

The approved operational requirements define controls for source capture, claim validation, contradictory evidence, learning records, and controlled knowledge changes. Existing architecture also requires source teaching to remain distinct from inference and hypothesis.

These documents define requirements and boundaries. Their existence does not prove that the educational corpus has been processed in compliance with them. Frozen architecture C1–C7 and D0–D14 remains closed.

## 4. Status of the Three Educational Source Groups

### 4.1 ICT Premium Mentorship Core Content

**Disposition: PARTIAL — REGISTRY COMPLETE; EDUCATIONAL COVERAGE NOT ESTABLISHED.**

The 115-record registry is an established deliverable and must not be recreated without a demonstrated reason. It is not a completed educational knowledge base.

Outstanding work, to be considered only after this audit is closed and a subsequent activity is authorized:
- Verify official provenance and current accessibility using suitable primary-source evidence.
- Reconcile material registry discrepancies.
- Review actual educational content.
- Extract explicit teachings with traceable source references.
- Separate source teaching from interpretation, inference, and hypothesis.
- Record ambiguity, contradictions, limitations, and unresolved questions.
- Complete independent QC and coverage checks.
- Integrate only appropriately validated knowledge through existing TOS controls.

### 4.2 Mentorships

**Disposition: UNVERIFIED — COMPREHENSIVE COVERAGE NOT ESTABLISHED.**

The evidence reviewed does not establish a complete Mentorships inventory, reviewed curriculum, completed teaching extraction, or coverage assessment. Before claiming completion, existing inventories and prior work must be located and assessed. Missing evidence must not be replaced by assumed coverage.

### 4.3 Latest Videos on Michael Huddleston’s Official Channel

**Disposition: UNVERIFIED — COMPLETE INVENTORY AND EDUCATIONAL REVIEW NOT ESTABLISHED.**

The agreed order remains **Core Content → Mentorships → Latest Videos**. The official Michael Huddleston YouTube channel remains the primary source. Secondary indexes may assist discovery and discrepancy investigation but cannot independently establish source authenticity or validate attributed teachings.

A complete inventory through a verified latest-publication cutoff is different from completing educational review of those videos. The available evidence reviewed for this audit does not conclusively establish the latest official-channel publication as of the audit date or complete coverage through that cutoff. Do not infer that a third-party listing is authoritative.

No automated or new study cycle is authorized by this finding.

## 5. Essential Gaps and Priority

### Priority 1 — Establish the Prior-Work Baseline

Locate and assess existing research outputs, learning records, extraction artifacts, QC reports, and knowledge-integration records.

**Why first:** prevents duplication of work that may already exist while ensuring undocumented or incomplete work is not treated as complete.

### Priority 2 — Establish Source and Educational Coverage

Preserve the agreed sequence:
1. Core Content.
2. Mentorships.
3. Latest official-channel videos.

For each group, distinguish source inventory, provenance, accessibility, actual content review, teaching extraction, evidence, QC, and integration.

### Priority 3 — Preserve and Reconcile Known Core Content Issues

Retain record 85, records 112–115 metadata concerns, the year-label conflict, the records 01–80 row-by-row limitation, and unresolved full-corpus availability/provenance verification. Close an item only when reviewable evidence supports closure.

### Priority 4 — Define and Evidence Educational Completion

Use existing TOS requirements to define observable coverage and acceptance evidence. Do not create a new architecture stage to address an educational coverage gap.

1. Relevant accessible prior artifacts and confirmed decisions have been reviewed.
2. Completed work is distinguished from partial, unverified, not-started, and blocked work.
3. Each of the three educational source groups has a separate evidence-based status.
4. Known registry limitations remain visible unless new evidence supports closure.
5. Essential gaps are prioritized without duplicating completed deliverables.
6. The report explicitly distinguishes completion of the status audit from completion of ICT education.
7. The report passes a consistency check and is presented to the Human Authority.
8. The final document is uploaded to GitHub and the Human Authority explicitly confirms the upload.

The audit may be complete while the educational workstream remains incomplete.
