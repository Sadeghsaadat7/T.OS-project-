## 1. Purpose

Stage 9 determines whether the approved Educational Agent design and the operational specifications established in Stage 8 provide a sufficiently controlled basis for proceeding to a bounded Prompt Engineering stage.

The assessment verifies, separately:

1. Whether the five approved Agents have sufficiently defined responsibilities and operational contracts.
2. Whether the inputs and evidence needed to design and later validate their prompts are identifiable.
3. Whether acceptance, handoff, stop, rework, escalation, and Human Authority controls are sufficiently specified.
4. Which prerequisites remain unresolved before practical prompt validation or operational educational execution.

This is a readiness verification. It is not execution of the Agents, validation of ICT educational content, or software implementation.

## 5. Readiness Dimensions

The following dimensions are assessed independently:

- **Specification readiness:** responsibilities, boundaries, inputs, outputs, stop conditions, and handoffs are defined.
- **Prompt-design readiness:** enough stable requirements and representative input context exist to design a bounded Agent prompt.
- **Practical-validation readiness:** a bounded task, representative accessible input package, and approved task-level acceptance criteria exist.
- **Operational execution readiness:** all necessary source, evidence, acceptance, dependency, and authority conditions are established for controlled educational execution.

A positive determination at one dimension must not be interpreted as a positive determination at another.

## 7. Agent-Level Readiness Assessment

### 7.1 A1 — Source Inventory & Reconciliation

**Specification readiness:** ESTABLISHED  
**Prompt-design readiness:** CONDITIONAL  
**Practical-validation readiness:** NOT ESTABLISHED  
**Operational execution readiness:** NOT ESTABLISHED

The responsibilities, source eligibility principles, and handling of source discrepancies are sufficiently defined to support bounded prompt design.

Before practical validation, a task-specific scope and identifiable, accessible candidate source references must be selected. Provenance, accessibility, integrity, and unresolved discrepancies must remain explicit. Existing verified records may be referenced as design context; this does not authorize operational execution.

### 7.2 A2 — Educational Review & Extraction

**Specification readiness:** ESTABLISHED  
**Prompt-design readiness:** CONDITIONAL  
**Practical-validation readiness:** NOT ESTABLISHED  
**Operational execution readiness:** NOT ESTABLISHED

The Agent's role and expected structured outputs are defined. Prompt design can proceed conditionally, but practical validation requires eligible A1 source records, accessible source material, and an approved bounded task scope.

Extraction completion must not be represented as evidence validation or independent QC acceptance.

### 7.3 A3 — Evidence & Knowledge Validation

**Specification readiness:** ESTABLISHED  
**Prompt-design readiness:** CONDITIONAL  
**Practical-validation readiness:** NOT ESTABLISHED  
**Operational execution readiness:** NOT ESTABLISHED

The validation responsibility and required evidence-oriented outputs are defined. Prompt design requires explicit task-level validation criteria and identifiable claims with supporting, contradictory, or insufficient evidence.

The Agent must preserve uncertainty and distinguish source teaching, evidence, and inference. It must not manufacture support where evidence is absent.

### 7.4 A4 — Independent QC & Coverage

**Specification readiness:** ESTABLISHED  
**Prompt-design readiness:** CONDITIONAL  
**Practical-validation readiness:** NOT ESTABLISHED  
**Operational execution readiness:** NOT ESTABLISHED

The independent QC role and reporting expectations are defined. Prompt design requires a clearly bounded review package, approved assessment criteria, and access sufficient to review the relevant upstream outputs.

A4 must remain independent of producing or correcting the work it evaluates. Its acceptance decision must be limited to the defined scope and evidence actually reviewed.

### 7.5 A5 — Knowledge Integration & Learning Record

**Specification readiness:** ESTABLISHED  
**Prompt-design readiness:** CONDITIONAL  
**Practical-validation readiness:** NOT ESTABLISHED  
**Operational execution readiness:** NOT ESTABLISHED

The controlled integration responsibility and required recordkeeping are defined. Prompt design can proceed conditionally, but practical validation requires eligible upstream outputs, valid A4 QC acceptance, approved integration rules, and Human Authority decisions wherever reserved authority is involved.

A5 must not bypass upstream acceptance conditions or autonomously convert unaccepted learning into approved operational knowledge.

## 8. Acceptance Evidence Readiness

Stage 8 defines the expected evidence and deliverables. Stage 9 finds that the evidence expectations are specified, but actual execution evidence has not been established.

| Agent | Required acceptance evidence | Stage 9 finding |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | Source identity, provenance, eligibility, accessibility, and discrepancy records | Requirements specified; execution evidence absent |
| A2 | Structured educational extraction with source locators and traceable records | Requirements specified; execution evidence absent |
| A3 | Claim-to-evidence links, validation outcome, uncertainty, and rationale | Requirements specified; execution evidence absent |
| A4 | Independent QC report, assessed scope, criteria, findings, and disposition | Requirements specified; execution evidence absent |
| A5 | Accepted knowledge records, source/evidence links, limitations, and integration/change history | Requirements specified; execution evidence absent |

Therefore, defined acceptance evidence must not be confused with evidence that the Agents have already produced accepted outputs.

The following gaps remain open:

## 10. Prompt Engineering Readiness Conditions

The five-Agent design and Stage 8 operational contracts provide a sufficient basis to begin a separately authorized, bounded Prompt Engineering stage, subject to the following constraints:

1. Preserve the approved five-Agent structure and all existing role boundaries.
2. Translate the Stage 8 contracts into Agent-specific prompt requirements without silently changing their meaning.
3. Explicitly encode required and optional inputs, expected outputs, stop conditions, handoffs, and escalation rules.
4. Ensure prompts handle missing, incomplete, inaccessible, conflicting, and ineligible inputs without inventing facts or silently treating them as valid.
5. Preserve A4 independence from the work it reviews.
6. Preserve A5's restriction to eligible upstream outputs and valid QC acceptance.
7. Retain Human Authority for reserved decisions, exceptions, and approvals.
8. Do not treat prompt completion as authorization for operational educational execution.
9. Do not introduce coding or software implementation under this readiness determination.

Practical prompt validation must remain a separately bounded activity with an approved task, representative input package, and task-level acceptance criteria. The unresolved gaps S9-G01 through S9-G03 must be addressed before that validation begins.

## 11. Final Stage 9 Determination

### Overall result

**CONDITIONALLY READY FOR BOUNDED EDUCATIONAL AGENT PROMPT ENGINEERING**

The approved five-Agent design and Stage 8 operational specifications provide a sufficiently defined basis for a separate, bounded Prompt Engineering stage. This is a limited readiness determination and is subject to the conditions in Section 10.

### Explicit limitations

- **NOT READY FOR OPERATIONAL EDUCATIONAL EXECUTION.**
- Practical prompt validation is not authorized by this determination.
- Coding and software implementation are not authorized by this determination.
- Educational coverage remains unestablished.
- Corpus-wide QC acceptance and controlled knowledge integration remain unestablished.
- Unresolved source and document-control limitations remain visible and must not be represented as resolved.
